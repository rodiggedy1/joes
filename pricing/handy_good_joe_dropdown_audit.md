# Good Joe Checkout Choices — Handy Comparison

**Benchmark market:** Washington, DC 20001  
**Checked:** September 3, 2026  
**Purpose:** Improve Good Joe’s customer choices without copying a competitor blindly. Handy’s current public forms are a structural benchmark; Good Joe retains additional safety and scope questions when they are needed to produce a responsible estimate.

> **Important:** Handy does not currently expose a direct public booking flow for lawn care, junk removal, or pressure washing. Its former plumbing page now routes to Angi quote matching. Those Good Joe inputs should be designed from the service itself and local market practice—not described as Handy choices.[1] [2]

## Quick Recommendation

Handy’s strongest pattern is simple: **quantity + time + notes**. Good Joe’s current forms have stronger scope protection, but several use broad category dropdowns where customers would answer more naturally with counts, duration, height, or property details. The recommended direction is to retain Good Joe’s safety questions while replacing vague options with clearer inputs.

| Good Joe service | Handy public form checked? | Best Good Joe direction |
|---|---:|---|
| Home cleaning | Yes | Bedrooms, bathrooms, duration, then service type/add-ons |
| TV mounting | Yes | TV count first; preserve Good Joe wall, mount, and cable questions |
| Furniture assembly | Yes | Small/medium/large item counts plus duration |
| Picture hanging | Yes | Small/large item counts, ladder height, shelves, duration |
| Minor home repairs | Indirect | Required job description, task count, duration, parts |
| Handyman visit | Yes | Required description, duration, parts/materials |
| Plumbing help | No current direct form | Keep problem, access, urgency, plus required description/photo |
| Electrical & lighting | Yes | Separate light/dimmer/fan counts, ladder height, duration |
| Interior painting | Yes | Keep Good Joe scope protection; add height and duration, not customer-selected pro count |
| Moving help | Yes | Helpers, hours, COI, boxes/materials, floors/elevator, notes |
| Lawn & yard care | No | Yard/property size, frequency, grass condition, requested work, access/haul |
| Junk removal | No | Volume, item category, pickup location, stairs/elevator, photo/parking |
| Pressure washing | No | Surface, approximate size, stain/condition, height, water access |

## What I Would Change First

The highest-value update is not simply adding more dropdowns. It is replacing generic choices with the few inputs that a customer can answer confidently and that actually affect the estimate.

| Priority | Service | Replace / add |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Furniture assembly | Small / medium / large quantity steppers and 30-minute duration blocks. |
| 2 | Home cleaning | Bedroom and bathroom counts plus duration; keep service type and add-ons after that. |
| 3 | Picture hanging | Small/large count, ladder height, shelves, and duration. |
| 4 | Electrical | Separate fixture/dimmer/fan counts, ladder height, and duration. |
| 5 | Moving help | COI, boxes/materials, floors/elevator, and duration; keep labor-only/no-truck. |
| 6 | Handyman and minor repairs | Required description plus duration, rather than broad job-type-only choices. |

## Pricing-Engine Implication

The current live estimate engine should remain intact. The implementation change is a mapping change: the new inputs replace the old field labels, then the estimate engine prices meaningful units such as **additional half-hour blocks**, **additional items**, **additional fixtures**, **helpers**, or **access conditions**. Safety-sensitive selections continue to result in **“Estimate · review required”**, rather than a fixed promise.

## Research Basis and Limits

This audit is based on Handy’s public Washington, DC forms checked on September 3, 2026. It observes only public pre-checkout fields; no order, payment, street address, or quote submission was completed. Handy currently gates dollar quotes after its contact/login flow, so this document is about **booking-choice structure**, not Handy price matching.
